# Steal a Slime — local saving implementation

Game Description:
Steal slimes from nurseries and place down on your plot to generate money

Upgrade your speed, bat, and other features, and fight other players for slimes using your slime bat. Generate more money as you rebirth and get better slimes, and upgrade your speed further.

How To Play:
- Load into the game, and automatically claim a plot to keep your slimes and earn money
- Buy upgrades for your speed with your money, and steal even better slimes to grow your collection
- Fight other players for rare slimes with your bat, hitting them to make them drop the slime
