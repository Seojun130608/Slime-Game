# Steal A Slime Project Description

## 1. Project Name

Steal A Slime

## 2. Project Overview

Steal a Slime is an open-ended Roblox collecting game where you capture slimes, escape giant guardians, and grow a valuable collection.

## 3. Why I Chose This Project

I chose this project because it incorporates many features of other popular games, which gives it a higher chance of a lot of people playing it. 

## 4. Target Users

The main target users are everyone that plays Roblox, but more specifically children.

## 5. Main Features
Capture: Use a Slime Grabber to pull wild baby slimes free. Your grabber must be powerful enough to capture the target slime, based on its hidden weight. Once captured, its giant parent chases you toward your base.

Player competition: Use a Slime Bat to interrupt another player’s capture or make them drop a carried slime. Anyone can pick up a dropped slime. Bat swings have a short cooldown, and players receive brief protection after being hit. You must put down a slime before swinging. Bases are safe zones.

Inventory and growth: Bringing a slime home secures it in your inventory as an ungrown baby. Hovering over it in your inventory reveals its weight. Placing it in your base starts its visual growth until it reaches the size determined by that weight. Growing changes its appearance, not its predetermined weight.

Random sizes: Each slime receives a random weight when stolen. Baby slimes look much smaller than their eventual grown size. No weight numbers or size labels appear above slimes in the world.

Income: Slimes placed in your base automatically generate cash. Their type, rarity, and weight determine their income. Bigger slimes of the same type earn more. Slimes stored in your inventory do not generate cash.

Fusion: Combine matching slime types at a Fusion Machine. Fusion consumes the original slimes and creates one of the same type and rarity. Their weights combine, determining the resulting slime’s grown size and increased income.

Upgrades: Buy Speed to run faster and Grabber Power to unlock heavier captures, pull slimes free faster, and reduce carrying slowdown.

Collection and rarities: Collect 38 slime types across ten rarities: Common → Uncommon → Rare → Epic → Legendary → Mythic → Exotic → Divine → Celestial → Secret. Each type has a fixed rarity. More dangerous areas offer better chances of finding rarer slimes.

Mystery Slimes: Mystery Slimes hide their type until you bring them home. Divine and Celestial mysteries share a soft white glow. Secret mysteries have a brighter white glow with drifting particles. Their weight is random, just like ordinary slimes.

Robux shop: Purchase a Premium Mystery Slime with a random weight and a guaranteed Epic-or-better result. It arrives safely at your base and cannot be intercepted. The shop shows the available rewards and their odds before purchase, with paid random purchases available only to eligible players. These are exclusive slimes, not able to be obtained through normal gameplay. Each rarity has one slime each.

Rebirth: Meet an increasing cash requirement to reset your cash, Speed upgrades, and Grabber Power upgrades while keeping your slimes and permanent unlocks. Each rebirth adds 0.5× to your total cash multiplier: 1.5× after one, 2× after two, 2.5× after three, and so on. Rebirths also raise upgrade limits and award Slime Tokens for extra base slots and cosmetics.

Open-ended progression: Keep discovering rare slimes, growing your collection through fusion, increasing income, and rebirthing. There is no final collection weight or intended last rebirth.



## 6. Art Style

Art style: Bright, simple Roblox visuals with rounded shapes and clean colors, some transparency
Slime bodies: Round dome shapes with flat bottoms, simple black eyes, and cute mouths.
Materials: Smooth, solid-colored bodies with subtle highlights.
Movement: Slimes bounce, wobble, squash, and stretch.
Slime types: Recognizable features such as leaves, mushroom caps, crystals, flames, or orbiting stars.
Baby slimes: Tiny versions that gradually grow to their predetermined size after placement.
Celestial slimes: Space-inspired colors, star markings, floating decorations, and gentle glow effects.
Giant parents: Enormous versions of their slime type with angry expressions and heavy bouncing movements.
Mystery Slimes: Dark bodies with floating question marks. Mythic–Celestial mysteries have a soft white glow; Secrets have a brighter glow and white particles.
Map: Colorful grasslands, glowing caves, candy valleys, volcanoes, floating islands, and cosmic areas.
Bases: Simple ranches with slime display spots, a Fusion Machine, and a clearly marked safe zone.
Equipment: A chunky Slime Grabber with a stretchy tether and a padded Slime Bat.
Fusion Machine: Two glass chambers connected to a central tank where slimes combine.
Interface: Large buttons, clear icons, and rarity-colored inventory cards. Weight appears when hovering over a secured slime in inventory.

## 8. First Version

- [x] One map: A grassy corridor with six open fenced player pens and five slime nursery zones.
- [x] Six slime types: Grass, Mud, Water, Honey, Crystal, and Gold, with rarities ranging from Common to Legendary.
- [x] Capturing: Use a Slime Grabber to pull babies free. Upgrade Grabber Power to capture heavier slimes.
- [x] Collection zone: Bring a carried baby to the entrance collection pad to secure it for the current session.
- [x] Chases: A giant parent chases you after a successful capture.
- [x] Player competition: Use a Slime Bat to interrupt captures or make players drop slimes. Bases are safe.
- [x] Inventory and growth: Secured babies stay in inventory until released into their pen. Hover to see weight; placed slimes grow and roam inside the fences.
- [x] Income: Slimes in the pen automatically generate cash based on their type and weight; stored slimes earn nothing.
- [x] Two upgrades: Purchase Speed and Grabber Power with earned cash. Each has five levels; Grabber Power unlocks heavier babies, shortens pulls, and reduces carrying slowdown.
- Rebirths: Reset cash and upgrades while keeping slimes. Cash multipliers progress through 1.5×, 2×, 2.5×, and beyond.
- Saving: Collections, cash, upgrades, and rebirth progress save between sessions.

## 10. Basic Program Flow

Player loads into game

Player goes to first slime

Player uses slime grabber to capture

Player runs back to collect zone

Player grows slime

Player places down fully grown slime into base

Player collects money

Player buys upgrades for speed and grabber
